Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026.

Horary casts a traditional horary chart for the moment you ask a question. This document says what the app actually holds, where it goes, and how to make it stop existing. It describes this app, not a category of app.

The short version

  • Your first question needs no account, no email address and no name.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics service and no tracking of any kind.
  • The one thing the app always needs is a rough location, because a chart without a place has no houses, and the houses are the answer.
  • The question you type is sent to a language-model provider, so it can be read back to you and narrated.
  • Everything we hold can be deleted from inside the app, and the deletion is real.

Who you are to us

On first launch the app generates a random identifier and stores it in the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore-backed store. It is not your device's identifier and not an advertising identifier, and it carries nothing about your phone: no model, no operating-system version.

It is the name of a row on our server, and that row owns your readings and your paid balance. Two consequences are worth knowing:

  • We cannot connect that row to you by name, because we never asked for one.
  • If the identifier is lost — a new phone with no restore, storage cleared — the readings and the paid balance attached to it cannot be recovered by us, because there is nothing else that proves they were yours.

Location

A horary chart is cast for a place as well as for a moment. The house cusps come from the latitude and the local sidereal time, and a chart cast for the wrong city answers a different question.

  • The app asks the operating system for reduced accuracy, which is city level. On Android it declares coarse location only. It never asks for a precise fix, because a metre of precision would change nothing in the chart.
  • The coordinates a chart was cast for are stored with the question. They have to be: without them the figure cannot be reproduced, and a reading that cannot be reproduced is not a record of anything.
  • We never ask for, and never store, a street address.
  • If you refuse location you can still read everything already in the app. You cannot cast a new figure.

The question you write

Your question is stored with the reading it produced, so your history can show it back to you.

It is also sent to a third party. There are two model calls per question, and both receive the text you wrote:

1. Reading it back. A language model restates the question as a will-it-happen question, separates it if it asks about two matters, and assigns the astrological house. That is what the confirmation screen shows you before anything is judged.

2. Narrating the verdict. After our own engine has judged the figure, a language model writes the verdict up in your language. It receives the verdict and your question and nothing else about the chart.

The provider is Anthropic, PBC, which processes the text on our behalf in order to return an answer. Our system is also built to run on OpenAI's models; if we change provider, this document is updated first. Neither is given a name, an email address or your location.

The judgement itself is not made by a language model. It is computed by a deterministic rule engine from the chart, and the narration is checked against the verdict afterwards: a sentence naming a planet or a degree that is not in the figure is rejected, and the reading is served without prose rather than with invented astrology.

Birth details, if you give them

Your name, birth date, birth time and birth place are optional everywhere in the app. They unlock the numerology and natal layers, and they never touch a horary verdict — that comes from the chart cast at the moment you asked.

  • Birth places are looked up on our own server, from a file that ships with it. The place you type is not forwarded to a mapping or geocoding company.
  • Each field can be removed on its own, or all of them at once, which also drops the numbers computed from them.

What else is stored

  • Readings: the verdict, the chart it was computed from, the testimonies, and the narration.
  • Outcomes you record — whether what the chart said actually happened — and any note you attach.
  • Reports you file about a reading that was wrong or harmful, with any note.
  • Your seal balance and subscription state.
  • Your language, so that a stored reading reopens in the language you read in today.

If you turn notifications on, the server plans them in advance and keeps a record of what it sent, so it does not say the same thing twice. Notifications are off until you turn them on.

How long

Until you delete it. There is no expiry job and nothing is archived elsewhere: a reading you asked for two years ago is still there because you may want to reopen it.

Deleting everything

Menu, then Delete my data. It deletes your questions, your readings, the outcomes you recorded, your birth details, anything queued to be sent to you, and the row that owns them.

It also deletes your paid balance, and any seals you have bought are forfeited. The app says so on the confirmation screen, because a deletion that quietly keeps the paying part of an account is not a deletion.

The server answers with counts of what it removed rather than with an acknowledgement, so the promise can be checked rather than trusted.

Birth details alone can be removed without deleting the rest, from the profile screen.

Payments

Seals and subscriptions are bought through the platform's own in-app purchase system, which is the App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by the platform. We never see or store a card number.

What we store is the result: how many seals you have, whether a subscription is active, and when it expires.

Not sold, not shared

We do not sell what we hold and we do not share it for advertising. The only third party that receives content you wrote is the language-model provider named above, and only in order to answer the question you asked.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect anything from a child under 13.

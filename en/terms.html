What Horary is

Horary is traditional horary astrology, computed rather than improvised. When you ask a question the app casts a chart for that moment and that place, a deterministic rule engine judges it under a named tradition, and a language model writes the judgement up in your language without adding to it.

What it is not

It is not advice. Nothing here is medical, legal, financial or psychological advice, and it is not a substitute for a professional who can be held responsible for what they tell you.

Astrology is not a science and this app does not claim otherwise. Readings are offered for reflection and entertainment. The decisions you take remain yours.

Some questions the app will not answer at all — questions about a death, a diagnosis, or someone else's private life. When it declines it says so, and no seal is spent.

Asking

  • Your first question needs no account.
  • A figure answers a question once. Asking the same question again within the repeat window returns the earlier reading rather than a second opinion. You may override that if the situation has genuinely changed, and you will be asked to say what changed.
  • The moment of a question is stamped by the server, not by your device. You cannot choose the moment a chart is cast for.
  • A chart that cannot be judged costs you nothing. When the engine refuses, no seal is spent.

Seals and subscriptions

A seal is what one judged question costs.

  • Seals and subscriptions are sold through the App Store or Google Play, at the price the store shows you for your country.
  • A subscription renews automatically at the end of every period until you cancel it. The store charges the renewal within 24 hours of the period ending.
  • To cancel, use the subscription settings of your platform account: on iOS, Settings, your name, Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store's Subscriptions screen. Cancelling stops the next renewal and the current period runs to its end. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • Refunds are the platform's to give, under the platform's own rules. We cannot refund a store purchase on the store's behalf.
  • Seals and subscriptions have no cash value, cannot be transferred, and belong to the identifier described in the Privacy Policy.
  • Deleting your data forfeits any balance you have paid for. The app says so before it does it.

You are responsible for what you type. Do not put other people's confidential information into a question.

Availability

The app depends on a server and on a third-party language model, and either can be unavailable. When the model cannot write, the reading is still served — the verdict is computed rather than generated — but the prose may be missing.

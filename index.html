Horary: Ask Astro

Support and frequently asked questions.

What the app does

Horary is the branch of astrology that answers a question rather than describing a person. You ask one thing; the app casts a chart for the exact second you asked it and a rule engine judges that question from that chart. Every testimony is shown together with the classical rule it came from, so the reasoning can be followed rather than taken on trust.

Planetary positions are computed from NASA JPL ephemeris data. The app is available in English, Turkish, German, Spanish and French, including the reading itself.

Frequently asked questions

Why did the app refuse to answer my question?

Some questions are declined on purpose: illness or diagnosis, death, pregnancy or fertility, the outcome of a court case, whether an investment will rise, and another named person's private life. A chart is not evidence about any of them, and a confident wrong answer there does real harm. A declined question is never charged for, and the app suggests a question it can answer instead.

What does “no judgement” mean?

Sometimes the figure carries a stricture — the classical rules saying that this particular chart cannot be read. The app tells you so plainly instead of inventing an answer, and the question is not spent.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Horary → Cancel Subscription. Cancelling stops future renewals; the period you have already paid for runs to its end. Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so cancellation and refunds are handled there rather than by us.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the menu, then Seals and layers, then Restore purchases.

How do I delete my data?

Menu, then Delete my data. That removes your questions, your readings, your birth details and anything queued for you. There is no account to close, because there was never an account to open.

I changed phone and my readings are gone.

The app has no login. A random identifier kept in the iOS Keychain owns your readings and your paid balance. Restoring the new phone from a backup brings it across. Without a restore we cannot recover it, because there is nothing else on our side that identifies you.

Is a reading advice?

No. Horary: Ask Astro is offered for entertainment and self-reflection. A reading is not a guarantee and is not a substitute for professional medical, legal, financial or psychological advice. The decision is always yours, and the app says so on every reading it gives.

Something is wrong, or a reading looks incorrect.

Write to the address below and include the date and time of the reading and the language you were reading in. Every reading stores the chart it was judged from, so a specific figure can be looked at.

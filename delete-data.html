Deleting your data

Horary: Ask Astro — published by Mehmet Emre Karamustafa.

How to request deletion

  1. Open Horary: Ask Astro on your phone.
  2. Tap the menu button in the top left corner.
  3. Choose Delete my data.
  4. Confirm. The deletion happens immediately and the app tells you how many records were removed.

No account, email address or support ticket is involved, because the app has no account to begin with.

What is deleted

Everything the service holds about you, on the server and on the device:

  • every question you asked, and the chart cast for it;
  • every reading, its judgement and the testimonies behind it;
  • outcomes you recorded, and any report you sent about a reading;
  • your profile — name, birth date, birth time and birth place — and anything computed from it;
  • your daily allowance record;
  • the identifier your device is known by, and the user row itself;
  • any notification still scheduled or queued for you.

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing personal is kept. There is no soft delete, no archive copy and no retention period: the rows are removed when you confirm, not marked hidden.

Two things survive, and neither identifies you. Ordinary web server logs, which record that a request was made, are kept for a short operational period and are not joined to your data. And our database provider takes encrypted backups on its own schedule, which expire on that schedule; no single reader can be addressed inside them, and nothing is restored from them to bring deleted rows back.

If you have already uninstalled the app

Delete your data before removing the app, if you can. The only thing that identifies your records is a random identifier held on your device, so once the app is gone we have no way to tell which rows were yours — and no way to confirm a request is really from you. What remains is a set of rows nobody can reach, tied to an identifier no device holds any more.

If you reinstall from a backup that preserves it, the identifier comes back and so does the option to delete.
